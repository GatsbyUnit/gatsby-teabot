Privacy

What the Gatsby Rotas site, the tea page and the tea bot keep about you, who sees it, and how to get it changed or removed. The tea system is run by members of the Unit (the tea tsar and the rota tsars), not by a company.

What the tea bot keeps

  • You on the rotas: your name, photo (if one was added), which rotas you are on, when you joined, and whether you are exempt.
  • Your duties: the talks, journal clubs, teas and coffee weeks you were or are down for, with talk titles and descriptions. Past slots stay as the rota's history, which is what keeps turns fair.
  • Your email address, for reminders, swap emails and your sign-in links.
  • Away dates you enter, with the reason if you give one.
  • Undo links for swaps (who handed which slot to whom) until used or the slot has passed, and who passed a slot on with "Can't make it" (so it doesn't come back to them), until the slot has passed.
  • Cancellations: a cancellation waiting for a tsar's signature (with its reason) until it is answered or the slot has passed, and how many of your own duties you have cancelled. That count is only ever shown as a rough rank with random noise added ("top 20% of cancellers"), in the announcement of your next cancellation.
  • A log of what the bot did and of changes made on the site (with the name of who made them), so mistakes can be traced and undone.
  • Diagnostics from your browser, without anything that identifies you (below).

All of this lives in Google Sheets, Google Drive and Apps Script of the tea bot's Google account, and in backup copies there. The bot's code is public on GitHub; the data is not.

Who sees it

  • Everyone signed in to the site (members of the Unit, and colleagues they have invited) sees the rotas: names, photos and duties. Email addresses appear there only masked (like ad•••e@u•••.ac.uk), and away dates not at all.
  • The gatsby.tea calendar shows names in its event titles, to everyone it is shared with.
  • The tsars edit the rotas; the tea tsar can also look at the bot's sheets and log, and open a read-only view of a member's tea page to help with a problem (with addresses, away dates and codes hidden; each use is logged).
  • Rota emails go to the people on duty, usually with the organiser in copy; group emails go to the Unit's mailing lists.

Services involved

The pages are served by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, sees your IP address when you load them (see GitHub's privacy statement). Everything the pages ask the tea bot goes to Google (Apps Script), and the bot's data, calendar and emails are Google services. No advertising, no third-party analytics, no cookies.

Stored in your browser

Only in this site's local storage, never sent anywhere by itself: your sign-in (for six months; "Sign out" removes it), the last tea page you opened ("Forget me on this browser" removes it), how long the bot usually takes to answer (for the loading bars), photos and the page icon (to show them at once), and your choice below.

Diagnostics

To find and fix problems (a page that hangs, a call that fails), the site and the tea page send the bot a few facts about each visit:

  • which page or view was open, by its name only ("TNJC", "a slot", "your tea page"), never which slot or person;
  • for each question to the bot: its name, how long it took, and whether it worked, timed out or failed (with the bot's error message);
  • script errors in the page;
  • browser and system (e.g. "Safari 18", "iOS"), phone / tablet / desktop, the page's version, and whether someone was signed in (member / invited / not).

Never sent: names, person or slot ids, personal codes, email addresses, or the page's address. Ids, codes and addresses are removed from error messages before they leave your browser, and again by the bot. A visit gets a random id that lasts only until you leave or reload the page, so visits can't be linked to each other or to you. The bot keeps these rows for 30 days, then deletes them.

Leaving, and your data

When you leave the Unit, "Leave the tea system" at the bottom of your tea page (or of any bot email) takes you off the rotas and removes your email address and personal code; your name stays in the rota history. To see what is kept about you, correct it, or have something removed, ask the tea tsar ("Report an issue" at the bottom of every page names them once you are signed in, or message them on Slack).
